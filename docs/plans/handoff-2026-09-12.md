## 5 — Tools, and what each is for

All in `tools/`, none importable by the package (`pyproject.toml` packages
`src/fpl_cli` only), so nothing shipped can reach a diagnostic.

- **`fu53_minutes_oracle.py`** — substitutes realised minutes and re-scores.
  **An oracle**: refuses an unsettled gameweek, must never be wired into the CLI.
- **`calibration_report.py`** — component error within a category bucket, and
  calibration by *predicted* bin. The second is the diagnostic that does not
  select on the outcome. Re-run after FU-57.
- **`optimiser_scale_probe.py`** — reads the recommendation's flip point off one
  run, since the objective is linear in the xP scale. Documents the two traps
  that cost two wasted runs (see §6).
- **`distribution_check.py`** — projections against the historical outcome
  distribution, norms baked in from the vaastav archive with no runtime
  dependency. [vaastav-survey.md](../research/vaastav-survey.md) is the source.

## 6 — Traps, each of which cost time once

- **A six-season reference is not automatically better than a two-gameweek one.**
  FU-67 was filed and refuted the same day: the reference pooled five pre-defcon
  seasons with one post-rule one, importing a bias larger than the effect being
  looked for — and looking authoritative because it rested on 227 gameweeks.
  **Defensive contribution arrived in 2025-26 and moved the defenders' share of
  points from 31.5% to 35.0%.** Use the post-rule reference.
- **A deep free-transfer bank makes any optimiser probe vacuous.** With enough
  banked transfers `hit_points` is 0 on every path, and per FU-64
  `terminal_value` is 0 as well, so both real-points terms are inert and nothing
  can be measured. Compounded by free transfers accruing *per intervening event*
  — overriding the count without also moving the state's `event` does nothing.
- **`plan evaluate` cannot pin fixtures or live.** Pin by hand; see §1.
- **An optimiser run is ~40 seconds** at horizon 3 on 650 players. Sweeping a
  parameter over six values takes four minutes and is usually avoidable — the
  objective is linear in the xP scale, so one run answers it.
- **Re-pinning a digest in `tests/test_variants.py` is almost never the fix.**
  The digests are cited as provenance in three documents.
